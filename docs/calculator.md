# add

## Signature

`add(a, b)`

Add two numbers and return their sum.

## Parameters

- `a` - The first number.
- `b` - The second number.

## Returns

The sum of a and b.

---

# subtract

## Signature

`subtract(a, b)`

Subtract the second number from the first.

## Parameters

- `a` - The first number.
- `b` - The second number.

## Returns

The difference between a and b.

---

# multiply

## Signature

`multiply(a, b, c)`

Multiply three numbers.

## Parameters

- `a` - The first number.
- `b` - The second number.
- `c` - The third number.

## Returns

The product of a, b, and c.
